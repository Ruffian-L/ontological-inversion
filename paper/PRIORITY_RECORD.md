## Evidence policy

Priority rests on dated repository commits, immutable hashes, run cards, raw
REPL transcripts, and paired research logs. paper/DISCOVERY_LEDGER.md records
positive findings with their original boundaries. paper/CHALLENGE_LEDGER.md
records later nulls and retractions. A challenge can narrow a discovery only at
the scope it directly tests. It cannot silently rename the earlier result, move
its date, or make authorship ambiguous.

Jason states that none of the academic papers cited in the manuscript influenced
the conception or construction of this work. Related work was added afterward
only to locate the finished contribution in the field. It is not an origin
story, a list of intellectual influences, or a collaborator list.
